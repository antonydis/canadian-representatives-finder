Privacy

InfoCivic uses your postal code to look up elected representatives. If you describe a situation in the guidance tool, your text is sent to our server for analysis and may be processed by an external AI provider. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

The site uses Google Analytics to measure visits and improve the experience. Language preferences may be stored in your browser.

Feedback submitted through the site may include your message, optional email address, representative name, postal code, and technical request information. Please share only what is necessary.

InfoCivic is an independent project, not a government website. This page is a plain-language overview, not a complete legal privacy policy.
