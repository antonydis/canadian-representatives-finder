About InfoCivic

InfoCivic is an independent civic information project for people in Canada who want to understand who represents them and which public institution may be able to help with a question or problem.

Enter a Canadian postal code to look up elected representatives, or describe a situation to identify a relevant level of government. InfoCivic helps you find a starting point; it does not act on behalf of a government or guarantee a resolution.

InfoCivic is not an official government website and is not affiliated with the Government of Canada.
